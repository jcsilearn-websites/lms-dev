# Permissions

> GENERATED by `pnpm docs:permissions` from `backend/src/core/authz` and the registered routes.
> Do not edit by hand: a test and CI fail when this file is out of date.

Authorization is checked by **permission name** with `can(subject, permission, resource?)`: deny by default, pure and synchronous for account-level checks (no database). Route checks are coarse; services re-check resource-level permissions and are the source of truth.

## Who can do what

Account roles are `platform_admin` (admin host only), `institution_admin` and `member` (tenant host). Course roles come from a participation in a course (`instructor`, `assistant`, `learner`) that the caller resolves and passes to `can()`.

27 permissions. Notes:

- An institution admin has read-only `content.view` on every course of the college but edits, publishes, creates assessments and grades only as a participant of that course.
- A session belongs to one host: a platform admin cannot act on a tenant host, and tenant users cannot act on the admin host.
