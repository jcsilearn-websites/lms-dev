# Data retention and erasure (step 14b)

What the platform keeps about a person, for how long, and what an erasure does. The wording of the erasure screens, of the
e-mails and of the preview is taken from this page; if one changes, change both.

**For the client's counsel (flagged, not decided):** the lines marked **COUNSEL** below are legal questions, not engineering ones.
The platform implements the rule written here; a different rule is a change to the retention functions, not a configuration.

## Two levels

| Level               | Who asks                              | Who decides               | What it touches                                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **College** (built) | the member, with their password again | another institution admin | the member's identity and personal data IN THAT COLLEGE. The global account and every other college are untouched. |
| **Account** (next)  | the member, or a platform admin       | a platform admin          | the global account (name, address, credentials), allowed only when no active membership is left. Not built yet.    |

## The request

1. The member sees a preview (counts only: how much is removed, how much is kept) and asks, entering the password again. A wrong
   password is counted like a failed sign-in.
2. Another institution admin approves or rejects. Nobody approves their own request. If nobody decides within **30 days** the request
   ends and the member is told, in the bell and by e-mail.
3. After the approval there are **7 days of grace**. The member or an administrator can cancel until the grace period ends.
4. After that the worker carries it out, in one transaction, and writes an entry (ids only) into the erasure ledger.
5. The last active institution admin of a college cannot be erased: refused when asking, when approving and when executing.

## What an erasure inside a college does

| Data                                                                | Treatment                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Display name                                                        | replaced everywhere in the college by a random pseudonym ("Former member 3F9A21C4"); the member's address is shown empty                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Membership                                                          | becomes `removed`; the member can no longer sign in to this college; nothing is ever sent to them again                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Sessions in this college                                            | all revoked in the same transaction; the address and agent they recorded are wiped                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Invite and reset tokens of the membership                           | deleted                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Profile: phone, designation, register number, employee id           | removed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Notifications, notification preferences, activity log (days active) | deleted                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Queued e-mail                                                       | skipped, never sent                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| E-mail rows (outbox) of the member                                  | address and parameters blanked (the row stays as a count)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Provider events of those e-mails                                    | address removed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Staged import rows that carry the address                           | scrubbed (they are in any case deleted 7 days after the import ends)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Data export archives of the member                                  | expired; the files are deleted by the purge job                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Audit trail                                                         | **kept**; only addresses are removed from the rows about the member (`ip`, `email`, `toEmail`, `address`, `recipient`, and the phone, designation, register number and employee id inside the `before` / `after` of their profile rows); nothing is deleted or rewritten otherwise                                                 |
| Suppressed address (an address that bounced)                        | the **keyed hash** (HMAC-SHA256 with a key of the deployment) is stored beside it. The plain address stays while the global account exists (the address is still in use: it can be invited from another college and must stay suppressed); account-level erasure replaces it by an unmatchable placeholder, leaving only the hash. |

## What is kept, and for how long

| Data                                                                                         | Kept                                                                             | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Academic record**: enrolments, hand-ins, quiz attempts, grades, progress                   | as long as the college keeps its academic records (the college's own rule)       | it is the college's record of what a student did; shown under the pseudonym. **COUNSEL:** the period.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Approved gradebooks** (frozen versions and their lines)                                    | as long as the academic record                                                   | frozen on purpose and immutable (append-only for everyone). **Each line keeps the name and register number it recorded when it was approved.** The list of what an approval froze is the college's academic record. **COUNSEL:** whether a name and register number in a frozen gradebook may stay, for how long, and whether the college wants them replaced after a period. |
| **Audit trail**                                                                              | as long as the college keeps its audit log; never deleted                        | accountability: who did what, when. Addresses are removed (above).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Backups**                                                                                  | Cloud SQL point-in-time recovery **7 days**; logical dumps **30 days**           | a backup is a copy of the database as it was. **After any restore the erasure ledger is applied again** (`pnpm privacy:reapply-erasures`), so an erased person is erased again.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **The erasure ledger**                                                                       | indefinitely (ids only: request, membership, user, college, level, time)         | the proof that an erasure happened and the means to repeat it after a restore. It holds no name and no address.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **E-mails already delivered**                                                                | not under the platform's control                                                 | they are in the inboxes they reached and at the e-mail provider (see the provider's own retention).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Data export archives** (a member's own download)                                           | **7 days** after they are prepared, then deleted                                 | convenience for the member only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Sessions, tokens, rate-limit counters, finished jobs, staged imports, read notifications** | the retention of each purge job (`PURGE_RETENTION_DAYS` 30, imports 7 days, ...) | operational data, deleted in batches.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

## What the platform will not do

- It does not rewrite the audit trail or the frozen gradebooks to remove a name; that is a decision of the college and its counsel.
- It does not erase another college's data about the same person, and a college cannot change what another college sees.
- It does not reach into backups; the ledger repeats the erasure after a restore instead.
- It does not claim that an e-mail that was already delivered can be recalled.
